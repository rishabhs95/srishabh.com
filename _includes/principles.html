Approach

How I manage

01

I work to not be needed

If I'm the bottleneck, something's broken. People on my teams own their work end to end, and there's always someone who could cover for me — ideally someone who could replace me.

02

I write things down

Half the disagreements I've seen were two people agreeing loudly. Plans live in docs that anyone can read, challenge, and point at six months later.

03

Hiring is the job

I've sat on hundreds of interviews. One good hire lifts a whole team; one bad one costs you two. I'd rather leave a seat empty than fill it badly.

04

Ship things that matter

Velocity charts don't pay anyone's salary. I care whether what we shipped moved the business — at Yelp that meant growing acquisition revenue from $45M to $60M, not a prettier burndown chart.
