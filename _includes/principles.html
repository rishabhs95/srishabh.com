Approach

How I operate

01

Leverage over heroics

My job is to make myself progressively unnecessary. Delegation with context, decisions pushed to the edge, and a succession plan for every critical role — including mine.

02

Strategy is written

Multi-quarter direction lives in documents, not hallways. I align teams on the why before the what — and let engineers own the how.

03

Raise the bar, then the people

Talent density compounds. I hold a high hiring bar and back it with sponsorship: stretch scope, honest feedback, and visible wins for the people doing the work.

04

Outcomes, org-wide

Teams are measured in business impact, not ticket velocity. I partner across product, design, and executive stakeholders so the roadmap moves the company — not just the codebase.
