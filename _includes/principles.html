What I bring

Four things I'm good at

01

My teams are measured in revenue, not story points. I've owned a $60M revenue line and a $2M budget, and I plan roadmaps the way a general manager would — what we build has to move a business number.

02

Teams that outlast me

I hire carefully, write plans down, and build feedback systems so the team runs fine when I'm on vacation. If I'm the bottleneck, something's broken — half my job is making sure someone could replace me.

03

Still technical

04

Influence past the org chart

Executive stakeholders, cross-org programs, conference stages, community meetups — the job doesn't stop at my two teams, and the interesting problems usually live between teams anyway.
